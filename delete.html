Delete your data

Effective date: 17 October 2026

You can ask us to delete the data we hold about you at any time. This page explains what we hold, how to ask, and what happens next.

What we hold, and what we delete

DataWhere it isDeleted when you ask?
Profile: nickname, role, gender, mobile number, email, organisation namesOur cloud databaseYes
Install ID, invite code, and the invite code you usedOur cloud databaseYes
Usage events (app opened, task created or completed)Our cloud databaseYes. Events are linked to your install ID, so they are removed with it.
Feedback you sentOur cloud databaseYes
Tasks, people, projects, schedule, progress and imported calendar eventsOnly on your phone (we do not have them)You delete them yourself: uninstall the app or clear its data in Android settings
Backup files you createdWherever you saved themYou delete them yourself

Task text and voice audio are processed to answer your request and are not stored by us.

How to ask

  1. Open the app, go to Settings, and tap Install ID to copy it. This helps us find your record quickly. If you have already uninstalled the app, you can skip this step.
  2. Email founder@samparkasetu.com from the email address you signed up with. Use the subject Delete my nounce ai data and paste your Install ID in the message.
  3. We may reply to confirm it is you. We will not ask for passwords.

What happens next

  • We delete your cloud data within 30 days and confirm by email.
  • Copies in routine backups are removed within 90 days.
  • We may keep the minimum needed to meet legal obligations or resolve disputes. We will tell you if that applies.
  • After deletion the app on your phone still works, but your next signup will create a new profile.

Other requests

To see, correct or export your data, or to complain, email founder@samparkasetu.com. See also our Privacy Policy.
