Privacy Policy: nounce ai

Samparkasetu Software Solutions Private Limited ("we", "us") provides the nounce ai mobile app. This policy explains what we collect, why, who we share it with, and your choices. It is written to follow Google Play's User Data policy and India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

1. The short version

  • Your tasks, people, projects and notes stay on your phone. We do not store them on our servers.
  • To understand a task you type or say, its text (or a short audio clip) is sent to AI providers, only for that request.
  • We keep a small account record (nickname, role, organisations, gender, mobile, email) and basic usage counts so we can run and improve the pilot.
  • You can ask us to delete your data at any time.

3. Who processes your data

  • Supabase (database and server functions, region: region: ap-northeast-2, Seoul).
  • OpenAI (reads task text to extract person, project, date, organisation).
  • Groq (speech-to-text for voice).
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging by Google (only if and when push notifications launch) (to deliver notifications; added when notifications launch).

These providers process data on our behalf. They have their own retention rules (for example, AI providers may keep API data for a limited period for abuse monitoring). See their policies: OpenAI (openai.com/policies), Groq (groq.com/privacy-policy), Supabase (supabase.com/privacy).

4. Permissions

  • Microphone: only while you use voice capture.
  • Notifications: reminders, a daily plan, summaries, an occasional tip or offer, and a daily community number (for example how many people finished tasks yesterday, made from counts only). Reminders are created on your phone from your tasks and are not sent through our servers. You choose which kinds in Settings and can turn them off in Android settings. Quiet hours default to 10 PM to 7 AM and you can change them in Settings. To time your morning plan we keep, on your phone only, the time you first open the app each day.
  • Internet: to reach the AI and our servers. Tasks still work offline.

5. Retention

Account record: until you ask us to delete it (or 24 months after you stop using the app). Usage events: 12 months. Feedback: 24 months. Data on your phone: until you uninstall the app or clear its data. Uninstalling removes your tasks, so use Settings > Back up my data first.

6. Your rights and choices

You can access, correct or delete your data and withdraw consent. Email founder@samparkasetu.com or use the in-app feedback screen (Settings > Send feedback), marked "delete my data". We reply within 30 days. You can edit your profile in the app. To delete everything on your phone, uninstall the app.

7. Security

Data travels over HTTPS. Database access is restricted so each phone can only read and write its own record. No system is perfectly secure; we will tell you and the authorities if a breach affects you, as the law requires.

8. Children

nounce ai is for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

9. Transfers outside India

Our providers may process data in other countries, including South Korea and the United States. We rely on their contractual protections.

10. Changes and contact

We will post changes here and, for material changes, in the app. Questions or complaints: founder@samparkasetu.com. Grievance officer: Pournendra G, founder@samparkasetu.com.
