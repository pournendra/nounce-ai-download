Privacy Policy

Effective date: 17 October 2026

Samparkasetu Software Solutions Private Limited ("we", "us") provides the nounce ai mobile app ("the app"). This policy explains what we collect, why we collect it, who we share it with, how long we keep it, and the choices you have. It is written to follow Google Play's User Data policy and India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. It starts with the short version.

1. The short version

  • Your tasks, people, projects and notes stay on your phone. We do not store them on our servers.
  • To understand a task you type or say, its text (or a short voice clip) is sent to AI providers, only to process that request.
  • If you allow it, the app reads the calendars already on your phone (for example Google Calendar and Outlook) to show your events as tasks. This happens on your phone only. Your calendar is never sent to us or anyone else.
  • We keep a small account record (nickname, role, organisations, gender, mobile number, email) and basic usage counts so we can run and improve the app.
  • You can ask us to delete your data at any time. See the Delete your data page.

4. How we use your data

  • To provide the app: sign you in anonymously, process your tasks with AI, send reminders you asked for.
  • To keep the service safe and fair: limit daily AI usage, prevent abuse of invite bonuses.
  • To understand and improve the app: counts such as daily active users and tasks created.
  • To contact you about the app, using your email or mobile number.
  • To meet legal obligations.

5. AI processing

When you add a task, the text is sent to OpenAI to extract the title, person, project, date, time and organisation. When you use voice, a short recording (up to 60 seconds) is sent to Groq for speech-to-text. Both are used only to answer your request. We do not store your task text or audio. These providers process data on our behalf and have their own retention rules, for example keeping API data for a limited period to detect abuse. Under their current terms, data sent through their business APIs is not used to train their models by default.

6. Microphone

The microphone is used only while you use voice capture. The app cannot record in the background.

7. Calendar

Calendar access is optional. If you allow it, the app reads events from the calendars already on your phone, such as Google Calendar and Outlook, for the next 45 days. It only reads: it never changes your calendar, never signs in to Google or Microsoft, and never sends your events anywhere. Events appear as tasks, and birthdays and similar events get a celebration card. You can turn it off in Settings or in Android settings. Turning it off removes the imported events that are still open.

8. Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app shows reminders 30 minutes before tasks, a morning plan, an evening summary, overdue nudges, birthday and celebration reminders, one tip or offer a day, and a daily community number (for example how many people completed tasks yesterday, built from counts only). Reminders are created on your phone from your tasks and are not sent through our servers. You choose which types you receive in Settings, can set your own quiet hours (10 PM to 7 AM by default), and can turn everything off in Android settings.

9. Invite programme

If you share your invite code, we record that a new user joined with it so that you and your friend receive bonus AI usage. We do not see your contacts and the app does not access them.

12. How long we keep data

  • Account record (profile, organisations, install ID, invite details): until you ask us to delete it, or 24 months after you stop using the app.
  • Usage events: 12 months.
  • Feedback: 24 months.
  • Data on your phone: until you uninstall the app or clear its data. Uninstalling removes your tasks, so use Settings, Back up my data first if you want to keep them.

13. Your rights and choices

You can access, correct, update or delete your data, withdraw consent, and make a complaint. Edit your profile in the app, or email founder@samparkasetu.com. For deletion, see the Delete your data page. We reply within 30 days.

14. Security

Data travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Our database is set up so that each phone can only read and write its own record. No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects you, we will tell you and the authorities as the law requires.

15. Children

nounce ai is for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has given us data, contact us and we will delete it.
